About us

Local people, parts you can trust.

Fast Parts is a family-run independent motor factors, serving Northwich and Winsford since 1989.

30+years in business

Established 1989

Fast Parts have been in business for more than 30 years, building a reputation for quality, reliability and great service that you can trust. We’re proud to have lots of loyal customers who return time and time again, because they know they’ll get the parts they need fast, with the service they deserve.

Staff who’ve been with us for decades

Unrivalled experience

Many of our staff have been with us for decades, building a knowledge base you’ll find invaluable when it comes to getting the right parts, first time. These are not just shop assistants, they’re dedicated auto-engineers who know your vehicle inside out and are happy to share that expertise with you.

Local service, national stock

Small firm, huge range

Fast Parts gives you the best of both worlds, with the friendly, local service of a small company, backed by the vast stock of a leading national supplier, covering almost every make and model. This business model keeps our costs down, and we pass those savings straight to you.
