Cookie policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

In short

This website sets one cookie that it needs in order to work, which remembers your cookie choice. Analytics cookies are off unless you click "Accept analytics" on our banner. We do not use advertising trackers. You can change your mind at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website saves on your device. Some are strictly necessary for a site to work. Others, such as analytics cookies, need your consent before they are used.

How the banner works

The first time you visit, a banner asks you to accept or reject analytics, with equal "Accept" and "Reject" buttons. Nothing from Google loads until you accept. If you reject, any analytics cookies are removed. Our privacy notice explains what analytics records and how long we keep it.

Our consent record

Each time you accept, reject or change your choice, we save a short record so we can show that we asked you properly. It contains a random ID (the same one held in your fp_consent cookie, after the dot), the date and time, your choice, the version of this policy you saw and the page you were on. It does not contain your name, email address or IP address. We keep these records for 12 months and then delete them automatically. The privacy notice explains how to ask to see yours.

Controlling cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. See the ICO's guidance on cookies for how.
