Privacy notice

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by Fast Parts Motor Factors, trading as Fast Parts Northwich and Winsford. Registered office 171 Witton Street, Northwich, Cheshire. We are the controller of the personal information described in this privacy notice. You can contact us on 01606 330 555 or contact@fastparts.uk.com.

Cookies and analytics

If you click "Accept analytics" on our cookie banner, we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It records things like the pages you view, your device and browser type, your approximate location (worked out from your IP address) and how you found the site. Our lawful basis is your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer. We keep analytics data for 14 months.

If you reject analytics, or do nothing, no analytics cookies are set and nothing is sent to Google. We do not use advertising trackers or embedded maps. Fonts are hosted on this site itself, and map links open Google Maps only if you choose to click them. See our cookie policy for the full list of cookies.

Our consent record

When you accept, reject or change your choice about analytics, we also save a short consent record so we can show that we asked you properly. It contains a random ID (also held in your fp_consent cookie), the date and time, your choice, the version of our cookie policy you saw and the page you were on. It does not contain your name, email address or IP address.

Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in being able to show that consent was asked for properly. We keep these records for 12 months and then delete them automatically. To see yours, send us the ID from your cookie (the part of the fp_consent value after the dot) and we will look it up. Without the ID we cannot tell which record is yours.

Information you send us

If you use the parts enquiry form, we ask for your name, email address, vehicle registration and the part you need, and optionally your phone number and vehicle make & model. If you request a trade credit application we ask for your business name, your name, phone number, email address and town or postcode.

We use this only to answer your enquiry or deal with your request. Our lawful basis is that it is needed to take steps at your request before a sale or contract, or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries. The forms on this site are processed by Web3Forms, which is run from India and receives what you type and passes it to us by email.

We keep enquiries for [retention period, for example 12 months] unless you become a customer, in which case we keep records as our business needs and the law requires. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without asking you first.

Who else sees it

Our website host, Cloudflare, serves the pages, stores our consent records and may process technical information such as your IP address, as described in Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. The form service we use, Web3Forms, handles the details you submit through our forms so they can be emailed to us, as described in the Web3Forms Privacy Policy. If you accept analytics cookies, Google (Google LLC, which is based in the United States) also receives the usage details described above, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Contact us using the details above. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
